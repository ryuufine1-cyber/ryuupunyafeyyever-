ARP Romantic Page — updated
=============================

Open index.html in VS Code/Live Server or directly in a browser.

Features:
- 3-2-1 opening
- animated romantic particle background
- glowing heartbeat
- typewriter messages
- "Open my heart" reveals the romantic letter
- Spotify embed for Shape of My Heart
- Spotify iframe is configured with autoplay=1

Important:
Modern browsers often block autoplay with sound until the visitor interacts with the page.
The Spotify embed is configured to request autoplay, but the browser/Spotify may still require a Play click.
